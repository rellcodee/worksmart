# WorkSmart 🚀

> **Work smarter, not harder.**  
> A modern personal productivity companion built to help you master your time, accomplish your goals, and elevate your daily routine.

---

## 💡 About WorkSmart

Many people strive for self-improvement and better daily productivity, but struggle to stay organized amidst busy schedules. **WorkSmart** was created to bridge that gap. 

Designed with simplicity and efficiency in mind, WorkSmart empowers individuals to manage their time intelligently, eliminate distractions, and make meaningful progress every day—without feeling overwhelmed.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 📋 **Task Tracker**: Organize your workload effortlessly with dedicated support for both **Daily** and **Weekly** tasks.
* ⏱️ **Pomodoro Timer**: Boost focus and prevent burnout using built-in, customizable Pomodoro technique sessions.
* 📝 **Personal Notes**: Jot down quick thoughts, ideas, and detailed reminders whenever inspiration strikes.
* 📅 **Schedule Management**: Plan and structure your days and weeks seamlessly to keep your priorities on track.
* 🎨 **Simple & Modern UI/UX**: Enjoy a sleek, distraction-free interface crafted for a seamless user experience.
