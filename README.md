## 💡 About WorkSmart

Struggling to stay organized amidst a chaotic daily schedule? **WorkSmart** was built to eliminate distractions and streamline your daily workflow.

Combining a **Pomodoro Focus Timer**, **Task Tracker**, **Personal Notes**, and **Schedule Planner** into one unified, offline-first app, WorkSmart empowers you to accomplish meaningful work every day—without burnout.

> 🌟 **100% Offline & Private:** All your tasks, notes, and timers are stored locally on your device using SQLite. No account creation required.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* ⏱️ **Pomodoro Focus Timer**: Customizable focus & break intervals inspired by the Pomodoro technique to boost deep work sessions.
* 📋 **Daily & Weekly Task Tracker**: Manage your priorities with clear daily checklists and weekly task planning.
* 📝 **Personal Notes**: Instantly jot down ideas, quick thoughts, or detailed study reminders anytime.
* 🔔 **Smart Daily Reminders**: Local scheduled notifications (at 07:00 AM) with custom alarm sound (`notif.mp3`) to set your daily intentions.
* 📅 **Schedule Management**: Plan and structure your week ahead to ensure your goals stay on track.
* 🎨 **Dark & Modern UI**: Eye-friendly dark mode design `#23272E` paired with high-contrast orange accents for maximum focus.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Libraries

* **Framework**: [React Native](https://reactnative.dev/) with [Expo SDK 53](https://expo.dev/)
* **Routing**: [Expo Router](https://docs.expo.dev/router/introduction/) (File-based routing)
* **Language**: [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/)
* **Database**: [Expo SQLite](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/sqlite/) (Local database)
* **Notifications**: [Expo Notifications](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/notifications/) (Offline scheduled local notifications)
* **Icon & Branding**: Custom Adaptive Owl Icon Architecture
